Heritage & Craftsmanship

From Switzerland’s Deepest Vaults to Your Private Cellar

Headquartered in Melano on the shores of Lake Lugano since 1991, ARVI SA represents the pinnacle of fine and rare wine merchantry. We bypass secondary middlemen to source directly from premier Châteaux and Domaines across Bordeaux, Burgundy, Champagne, and Tuscany.

Every single bottle rests within our subterranean cellars under strictly regulated micro-climates (12°C constant temperature, 70% humidity) until dispatch. Whether you are building an investment-grade portfolio or securing an iconic vintage, our Swiss integrity guarantees authentic perfection.
